Privacy Policy

Product name: Converto

1. Who we are

Converto (“the App”) is a currency, cryptocurrency, and unit converter for iOS and Android. There is no account and no sign-in.

2. Summary

Data Stored Sent off device
Conversion history No No
Language, theme, selected units On device only No
Last FX / crypto rates On-device cache Downloaded from public market APIs
Advertising ID / tracking Not used No

3. What we do not collect

The App does not save conversion history (amounts, unit pairs, or timestamps). We do not collect name, email, payment data, advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera. No analytics, crash, or ads SDK is embedded at the time of this policy.

4. On-device cache

Language, appearance, selected currencies and units, keypad state, last downloaded FX and crypto rates, and optional scene images stay on your device so Converto can work offline with the last updated rates. Uninstall the App or clear App storage (Android) to remove this cache.

5. Network

When online, public market data is fetched over HTTPS from Frankfurter (ECB), ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com), a currency CDN (cdn.jsdelivr.net), CoinGecko, and Binance. Optional flag and scene images may load from CDNs. Those services may see standard request metadata such as IP address. The App does not send conversion history.

6. Children

The App is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

7. Legal bases (EEA / UK)

Where GDPR applies: Art. 6(1)(b) (the conversion you request) and Art. 6(1)(f) (offline last rates and preferences). We do not sell personal data.

8. Your rights

To delete App data, uninstall Converto or clear App storage. We do not run user accounts or conversion logs. California: we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

9. App Store & Google Play

No App Tracking Transparency tracking, no Advertising Identifier. Conversion history is not collected. Contacts, camera, microphone, and precise location are not required.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change.

11. Contact

There is no dedicated support email. Use the App Store or Google Play listing for Converto, or the in-app Privacy screen.
